About the Marathon

The Youth Marathon – 2nd Edition is being organized to mark the auspicious occasion of National Youth Day and the birth anniversary of Sri Swami Vivekananda. This event celebrates the ideals of strength, unity, discipline, and service that Swami Vivekananda stood for.

With every stride, we pay tribute to the visionary monk who believed that “Youth are the real nation builders.”

Our Mission

  • To encourage physical fitness, mental strength, and healthy lifestyles among youth.
  • To instill Swami Vivekananda’s teachings of discipline, self-belief, and service to society.
  • To create awareness on the importance of youth participation in nation-building.

Our Vission

  • To build an annual flagship marathon that stands as a symbol of youth power and collective responsibility.
  • To create a generation of inspired, confident, and socially responsible young leaders.
  • To promote a culture where fitness, unity, and service go hand in hand.
